A newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 17 November 2026 in one image. Drawn procedurally with PIL: a masthead, a red date band, six story panels and a ticker strip. The six stories, all reported on 17 November 2026: the Kerch Bridge span falls after a Ukrainian strike; Iranian drones target a US radar base in Bahrain and are intercepted; China confirms a Xi–Putin–Trump meeting at APEC in Shenzhen; five EU states plan a Rwanda migrant return hub; the US tightens its AI chip export ban on China; Japan scrambles fighter jets after Russian aircraft enter its airspace.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG). Deterministic: two runs write byte-identical output.
